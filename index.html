Weekly

Creative Brief

WHAT IS A CREATIVE BRIEF?

A creative brief is your content roadmap for the week. It outlines the themes, angles, hooks, and key messages for all posts across both brands. Use this brief to guide copy writing and image creation. You receive this from your manager every Monday morning.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Week of: Date range | Brand focus: ALM / AWG or split | Overall theme: Seasonal, campaign, product launch, etc. | Key angles: 3–5 content angles to explore | Hooks/CTAs: How to draw attention and drive engagement | Product/topic spotlights: What to feature | Post count: Number of posts per platform

EXAMPLE BRIEF

Week of May 27–June 2
Brand: ALM (primary) + AWG (1 post)
Theme: Summer Comfort Campaign

Angles:
• Comfort as a core value (maternity wear that doesn't sacrifice style)
• Real mom testimonials (Reels format)
• Style tips for each trimester
• Behind-the-scenes design process
• User-generated content showcase

Hooks: "Stop sacrificing comfort," "See how real moms style…," "You don't have to choose," "Watch how we made…"

Spotlight: New Summer Collection launch (Fri post)

Post count: ALM: 5 posts (Insta 2x, FB 1x, TikTok 2x) | AWG: 1 post (Insta)

Weekly

Cycle

MONDAY · RECEIVE BRIEF & PLAN

Receive creative brief from manager. Fill out the Creative Brief Template above. Review content calendar for both brands. Break down brief into individual posts. Create Asana tasks for all content. Set deadlines: copy due Wed, images due Thu, approval by Thu 5 PM.

TUESDAY–WEDNESDAY · CREATE COPY

Write captions using angles and hooks from the brief. Reference brand voice in Cheat Sheet. Captions ready for approval by Wednesday close of business. Move Asana tasks to "Ready for Approval."

THURSDAY · CREATE IMAGES & SUBMIT

Source or create images per brief specs. Verify they match brand aesthetic. Attach to Asana tasks. All content ready for approval by 5 PM Thursday.

THURSDAY EVENING · APPROVAL

Manager reviews and approves. If revisions needed, you make changes Friday morning. Once approved, posts move to "Ready to Post."

FRIDAY–NEXT WEEK · SCHEDULE & POST

Schedule approved posts using Meta Business Suite (Instagram/Facebook) or TikTok Creator Studio. Posts go live Fri–Wed per Posting Times table. Monitor engagement daily.

FRIDAY · REPORT & MONITOR

Check metrics on all live posts. Respond to comments. Compile weekly summary and share with manager by 5 PM.

Content

Creation

COPY WRITING

Use the creative brief's angles and hooks as your starting point. Open the relevant Cheat Sheet (ALM or AWG). Follow brand voice guidelines. Keep captions concise but engaging. Use approved hashtags only. Check grammar and tone. Save draft in Asana task description.

AWG tone: [Reference your Cheat Sheet]
Instagram: Up to 2,200 characters, hashtags end of caption or first comment | Facebook: 1,300 characters recommended | TikTok: 150 characters, hashtags critical

IMAGE SOURCING & CREATION

Use the brief's visual direction. Check Cheat Sheet for brand aesthetics. Use existing brand assets (Figma, Canva, photo library) or create new. Tools: Canva (templates), Figma (brand designs), stock photos, or original photography.

Image specs:
Instagram Feed: 1080×1350px | Instagram Stories: 1080×1920px
Facebook: 1200×628px minimum | TikTok: 1080×1920px vertical
File format: PNG or JPG | Brand colors/fonts only | No filters outside brand aesthetic

Posting

Process

1. WAIT FOR APPROVAL

Task must be "Ready to Post" in Asana before proceeding. If revisions needed, manager will add comments.

2. OPEN PLATFORM

Log into brand account: Meta Business Suite (Instagram/Facebook) or TikTok Creator Studio.

3. PASTE & UPLOAD

Create new post. Paste caption exactly as written. Upload image(s). Check preview for cropping/quality.

4. SCHEDULE

Select "Schedule" (never "Post Now" unless same-day emergency). Use time from Posting Times table. Confirm scheduling window (TikTok: 10 days max, Facebook: 6 months max).

5. LOG & COMPLETE

Return to Asana. Mark task "Complete." Add comment: "Scheduled [Platform] [Date] [Time]." Include post URL once live (add next day).

Engagement

& Reporting

DAILY MONITORING

Check all live posts daily. Respond to comments and messages per brand voice (see Cheat Sheet). Flag spam. Report issues immediately.

24-HOUR METRICS

After each post is live 24+ hours, add to Asana task comment:

Likes: [#]
Comments: [#]
Shares: [#]
Follower gain: [+#]

WEEKLY REPORT (FRIDAY 5 PM)

Post summary: Total posts published this week (by platform)
Engagement total: Combined likes + comments + shares (by platform)
Top post: Highest engagement (platform + title)
Growth: Follower count change week-over-week
Notes: Anything unusual or notable

Share in Asana comment and tag manager.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Image crops wrong? Check specs above. Try larger source file. Caption too long? Edit before posting—platform has character limits. Won't schedule? Check TikTok 10-day max or all fields filled. Questions? Tag manager in Asana task.

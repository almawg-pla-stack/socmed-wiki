Roles

& Responsibilities

📋 Creative Direction & Strategy
PSALM (PLA)

Responsibilities:

• Creates weekly creative brief with themes, angles, hooks, and post count
• Defines content direction and strategy for both brands
• Reviews all content created by Iris before posting approval
• Approves final content for Kathy to post
• Monitors performance and provides feedback
• Updates strategy based on engagement data

🎨 Content Creation
IRIS

Responsibilities:

• Receives creative brief from Psalm
• Creates all copy (captions, hooks, CTAs)
• Sources or creates all visual assets (static images, videos, graphics)
• Ensures content aligns with brand voice and aesthetic per Cheat Sheet
• Uploads content to Asana tasks for review
• Makes revisions based on Psalm's feedback

📱 Posting & Scheduling
KATHY

Responsibilities:

• Receives approved content from Psalm
• Posts and schedules content using Meta Business Suite (Instagram/Facebook) and TikTok Creator Studio

• Monitors comments and messages on all platforms
• Responds to engagement per brand voice guidelines
• Collects 24-hour metrics on all posts
• Compiles weekly performance report for Psalm

Weekly

Workflow

MONDAY · PSALM CREATES BRIEF

Psalm creates creative brief for the week covering both brands. Brief includes: theme, 3–5 angles, hooks/CTAs, product spotlights, post count by platform. Brief is shared with Iris in Asana by 10 AM.

TUESDAY–THURSDAY · IRIS CREATES CONTENT

Iris receives brief and creates all content. Writes copy using brief angles. Sources/creates images. Uploads to Asana tasks by Thursday 5 PM with all copy and assets attached. Reference Cheat Sheet for brand voice and visual guidelines.

THURSDAY EVENING · PSALM REVIEWS & APPROVES

Psalm reviews all content in Asana. If approved, moves tasks to "Ready to Post." If revisions needed, adds comments and moves back to "In Progress" for Iris to revise Friday morning.

FRIDAY–NEXT WEDNESDAY · KATHY POSTS & SCHEDULES

Kathy receives "Ready to Post" tasks. Posts and schedules content using Meta Business Suite (Instagram/Facebook) and TikTok Creator Studio at recommended times. Updates Asana with post URLs and scheduling confirmation.

DAILY · KATHY MONITORS ENGAGEMENT

Kathy monitors all platforms for comments and messages. Responds per brand voice guidelines (see Cheat Sheet). Flags issues or unusual activity for Psalm.

FRIDAY · KATHY REPORTS TO PSALM

Kathy compiles weekly report by 5 PM: posts published (by platform), total engagement (likes + comments + shares by platform), top post, follower growth. Shares in Asana and tags Psalm.

Every Monday morning, Psalm creates a creative brief that guides Iris's content creation for the week. The brief is the roadmap for all social media content.

Overall theme: [Campaign, seasonal, product, etc.]

• Angle 1
• Angle 2
• Angle 3

HOOKS & CTAs:
• Hook 1
• Hook 2
• Hook 3

Product/Topic spotlight: [What to feature]
Visual direction: [Style, mood, format notes]

POST COUNT:
ALM: __ Instagram, __ Facebook, __ TikTok
AWG: __ Instagram, __ Facebook, __ TikTok

EXAMPLE

Week of May 27–June 2
Brands: ALM (primary) + AWG (1 post)
Theme: Summer Comfort Campaign

Angles:
• Comfort without sacrifice (maternity style)
• Real testimonials from moms
• Styling tips per trimester

Post count: ALM: 2 Insta, 1 FB, 2 TT | AWG: 1 Insta

Content

Creation (Iris)

IRIS RECEIVES BRIEF & CREATES

Iris receives Psalm's brief Monday morning and begins creating all content. Use the brief's angles and hooks to guide copy writing and visual creation.

STEP 1: REVIEW BRIEF & BRAND GUIDELINES

Read Psalm's creative brief. Open the relevant Cheat Sheet (ALM or AWG). Familiarize yourself with brand voice, visual style, hashtags, and platform requirements.

STEP 2: WRITE COPY

Write captions using the brief's angles and hooks. Follow brand voice from Cheat Sheet. Keep copy concise but engaging. Include CTAs. Use approved hashtags only.

Character limits: Instagram 2,200 chars | Facebook 1,300 chars | TikTok 150 chars

STEP 3: CREATE/SOURCE IMAGES

Create or source images aligned to brief's visual direction. Use Canva, Figma, stock photos, or original assets. Follow brand specs from Cheat Sheet.

Image specs: Instagram 1080×1350px | Facebook 1200×628px | TikTok 1080×1920px (vertical)

STEP 4: CREATE ASANA TASK

Title: [BRAND] [DATE] [PLATFORM] — Example: "ALM 05.27 Instagram Post"
Description: Brief angle used | Copy | Images attached | Hashtags
Due: Thursday 5 PM
Status: Move to "Ready for Approval" when complete

STEP 5: REVISE IF NEEDED

If Psalm requests revisions Thursday evening, update content Friday morning and move task back to "Ready for Approval."

Posting

& Scheduling (Kathy)

KATHY POSTS APPROVED CONTENT

Kathy receives "Ready to Post" tasks from Psalm and schedules/posts content on all platforms.

STEP 1: CHECK ASANA

Review Asana for "Ready to Post" tasks. Grab the post time, platform, copy, and images.

STEP 2: OPEN PLATFORM

Instagram/Facebook: Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com)
TikTok: TikTok Creator Studio (studio.tiktok.com)

STEP 3: CREATE POST

Create new post. Paste caption exactly. Upload images. Check preview for quality and cropping.

STEP 4: SCHEDULE

Select "Schedule" (never "Post Now"). Use posting time from table below. Confirm scheduling window (TikTok: 10 days max, Facebook: 6 months max, Instagram: 1–3 days).

STEP 5: LOG IN ASANA

Return to Asana. Mark task "Complete." Add comment: "Scheduled [Platform] [Time]." Add post URL once live.

POSTING TIMES

Platform Weekday Weekend Frequency
Instagram 10:00 AM 9:00 AM 1–2x/day
Facebook 12:00 PM 11:00 AM 1x/day
TikTok 7:00 PM 7:00 PM 3–5x/week

ENGAGEMENT & MONITORING

Monitor all platforms daily for comments and messages. Respond per brand voice (see Cheat Sheet). After 24 hours, log metrics to Asana: likes, comments, shares, follower gain.

WEEKLY REPORT (FRIDAY 5 PM)

Post summary: Total posts by platform
Engagement: Combined likes + comments + shares by platform
Top post: Highest engagement
Growth: Week-over-week follower change
Notes: Anything unusual or notable

Share in Asana comment and tag Psalm.

Asana

Task Management

TASK STRUCTURE

Title: [BRAND] [DATE] [PLATFORM] — "ALM 05.27 Instagram Post"
Assignee: Iris (creation phase) → Kathy (posting phase)
Due: Thursday 5 PM (creation deadline)
Custom fields: Brand (ALM/AWG) | Platform (Instagram/Facebook/TikTok) | Post URL | 24h Engagement

STATUS FLOW

Not Started (Iris) → Writing copy, sourcing images
↓
In Progress (Iris) → Copy written, images selected
↓
Ready for Approval (Iris) → All content ready, Psalm reviews Thu evening
↓
Ready to Post (Psalm approves) → Kathy schedules & posts
↓
Complete (Kathy) → Posted + metrics logged

IF REVISIONS NEEDED

Psalm moves task back to "In Progress" with comments. Iris revises Friday morning and moves back to "Ready for Approval."
